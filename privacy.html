Privacy

Draft outline for the prototype. Needs legal review against the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles before launch.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile.
  • Driver verification: licence details, licence photos and a selfie, used to confirm identity and licence class.
  • Owner details: ABN, business details, vehicle registration, insurance documents and payout account.
  • Booking and payment records.

Why we collect it

To verify that drivers are licensed for the vehicle they rent, to verify owners and vehicles, to process payments and payouts, and to resolve claims and infringements.

Who we share it with

Owners see a renter's name, verified licence class and status for their own bookings. Identity checks are performed through a verification provider. Payment details are handled by a payment provider and not stored by TruckRentals.

This prototype

This preview stores what you enter in your own browser only. Nothing is sent to a server. Use the "Reset prototype data" button on the dashboard to clear it.
