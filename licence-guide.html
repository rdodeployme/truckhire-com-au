Licence guide

What can I drive?

Your licence class is based on the truck's GVM (gross vehicle mass, the most it can legally weigh fully loaded) and its number of axles. It's on the compliance plate, and on every TruckHire listing.

Upgrading your licence

Each state sets its own rules, but you generally need to have held a car licence for a minimum period (often 1 year for LR or MR, longer for HR and above), then complete training and a test with an accredited provider.

Check with VicRoads, TMR Queensland or your state's transport authority.

Auto-only conditions

If you sat your heavy vehicle test in an automatic or synchromesh truck, your licence may carry a condition limiting you to those gearboxes. Filter for Automatic when you search.

Over 12 tonnes GVM

Fatigue rules apply to trucks over 12 t GVM in states under the Heavy Vehicle National Law. You may need a work diary for trips more than 100 km from base. See NHVR.

Provisional licences

P-platers can hire car-licence trucks on TruckHire. Trucks needing LR or above require a full licence (proposed rule, confirmed at booking).

General guide only, not legal advice. Your state transport authority has the final word on licence requirements.
