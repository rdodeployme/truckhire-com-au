How it works

Simple on both sides

A marketplace connecting people who need a truck with owners whose trucks are sitting idle. Checked, priced upfront and protected.

Renting

From search to drop-off

  1. Search by job, size or licenceUse the size finder if you're not sure, and set your licence class to see only trucks you can drive.
  2. Check the full pricePick dates, estimate your kilometres and choose cover. The total is calculated before you commit.
  3. Verify your licence onceLicence details, photos and a selfie. The class is checked against the truck automatically.
  4. Book or requestInstant-book trucks confirm straight away. Others go to the owner, and you're only charged if they accept.
  5. Pick-up walk-aroundMeet the owner, show your licence, photograph the truck in the app. The bond is held on your card.
  6. Return it the sameSame fuel level, same condition, photos again. Bond released after a clean return.

Listing

From idle to earning

  1. Register your truckBusiness details, rego, specs from the compliance plate, insurance and photos.
  2. We verify itABN, registration, insurance and documents are reviewed before your listing goes live.
  3. Set your rulesRates, kilometres, bond, available days and whether to approve each request or allow instant book.
  4. Approve rentersEvery request shows the driver's verified licence class and your exact payout.
  5. Hand over & check inDo the photo walk-around together. Time-stamped photos protect both of you.
  6. Get paidPayout to your account after the rental is completed and checked back in.
