Trust built in

Every rental checked
on both sides.

Licence-checked drivers

Every renter's licence class, expiry and ID are checked. The booking won't go through unless their licence covers that truck.

Verified trucks & owners

ABN, registration, insurance and roadworthiness checked before a truck goes live.

Upfront pricing

Daily rate, included kilometres, cover and fees are all shown before you pay. No surprises at drop-off.

Secure payments & bond

Payment and bond handled through the platform. The bond is a hold, released after a clean return.

Photo check-in & out

Both sides photograph the truck at pick-up and return, time-stamped in the app. Disputes settled on evidence.
