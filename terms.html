Legal

Terms

Draft outline for the prototype. Needs to be written and reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

1. The marketplace

TruckRentals.com.au connects people who want to rent a vehicle ("renters") with people who own vehicles ("owners"). The rental agreement for each booking is between the renter and the owner, facilitated by TruckRentals.

2. Renter eligibility

  • Hold a current Australian licence of the class required for the vehicle.
  • Meet the minimum age and licence-history requirements shown at booking.
  • Pass identity and licence verification.
  • Only verified drivers named on the booking may drive.

3. Renter responsibilities

  • Load within the legal payload and restrain loads properly.
  • Follow road rules, and fatigue rules where they apply.
  • Return the vehicle on time, at the same fuel level and in the same condition.
  • Pay tolls, fines and infringements incurred during the rental.

4. Owner responsibilities

  • Keep the vehicle registered, roadworthy and insured to be rented out.
  • Provide accurate specifications, including GVM and axles.
  • Honour accepted bookings and hand over the vehicle as listed.

5. Payments, fees and bonds

Renters pay the booking total through the platform, including a service fee. Owners receive the rental amount less a commission. A refundable bond is held on the renter's card for the rental period.

6. Cancellations

Cancellation terms are shown at booking.

7. Damage and claims

Damage is assessed from check-in and check-out photos. Costs are recovered from the bond and the renter's cover, up to the applicable excess, except where the terms were breached.
