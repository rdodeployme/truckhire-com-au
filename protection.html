Trust & safety

Protection on every rental

Checks before the booking, evidence during it and a clear process if something goes wrong.

For renters

Choose the cover level that suits the job. The excess is the most you'd pay towards damage you're responsible for.

Cover tiers and prices are proposed and subject to confirmation with an insurance partner. Excess doesn't apply to damage from breaching the rental terms (e.g. overloading, unlicensed or impaired driving, or overhead damage from ignoring height clearances).

For owners

Only licensed drivers

Licence class, expiry and identity are verified, and the class is checked against your truck's GVM before a request can reach you.

Bond you set

Held on the renter's card at pick-up, released after a clean return. Used first for fuel, cleaning or minor damage.

Time-stamped photos

Walk-around photos at pick-up and return, stored against the booking, so damage claims rest on evidence.

Fines & tolls transferred

The booking record names the driver and the rental period, so infringements can be nominated to the renter.

You approve requests

Decline any booking. Instant book is optional and only for drivers who meet your rules.

Insurance

Your truck needs insurance that permits it to be rented out. Owner cover through TruckRentals is being finalised.

If something goes wrong

  1. Report it in the appBreakdown, accident or damage. Report it straight away with photos.
  2. We compare the photosPick-up and return photos are compared to confirm what's new.
  3. Bond, then coverMinor costs come from the bond. Larger damage goes through the renter's cover, up to their excess.
  4. Owner is paidThe owner is reimbursed through the claim and keeps their rental payout.
